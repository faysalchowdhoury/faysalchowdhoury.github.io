About

I am a Research Associate at the Rochester Institute of Technology, working with Dr. Lu Sun. I build physics-informed machine learning models for electric-vehicle charging demand, power-grid stability, and transportation safety, and I validate them on real-world networks.

I received my Ph.D. in Interdisciplinary Engineering (Electrical and Computer Engineering concentration) from Kennesaw State University in 2026, advised by Dr. Mahyar Amirgholy in the Futra Lab. My dissertation, Machine Learning for Predictive Energy and Emissions Modeling of Vehicles and Power Grids in the United States, led to TEEMAT, an open tool that predicts vehicle and grid emissions across the country.
